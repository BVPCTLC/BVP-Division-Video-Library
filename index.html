Welcome

One place for crew training and safety awareness

The Electrical TRO Department of Bhavnagar Division brings its training and safety awareness videos together in this library, so that every member of the running staff can learn, revise and refresh key safety practices at any time.

The videos cover important safety topics such as KAVACH, RS Valve, Look Back, Cattle Run Over and the Working Time Table, presented in simple language to make the message clear and easy to remember.
